Clearpane opens the websites you use as if they were apps, and keeps them clean.

<b>Blocking</b>
• Blocks requests to about 80,000 known ad, tracker and malware hosts
• Hides common ad containers and floating banner ads inside pages
• Refuses popups and new windows
• "Stay on this site" stops a page from redirecting you somewhere else

<b>Your sites, as apps</b>
• Favourites appear as a grid of tiles with each site's own icon
• Put any favourite on your home screen as its own icon
• Choose what opens at launch: your favourites, where you left off, or a start page

<b>Everyday browsing</b>
• Tap the site name to type an address or search with DuckDuckGo
• Audio keeps playing with the screen off
• File uploads and downloads, including files a page builds itself
• History grouped by site, searchable, with multi-select delete; or switched off entirely

<b>Privacy</b>
Clearpane has no account, no analytics and no servers of its own. Favourites, history and settings stay on your device. Third-party cookies are refused.

<b>Good to know</b>
• Blocking is by host name plus general page rules, so some ads get through.
• The optional YouTube ad skipper is best-effort and is against YouTube's terms of service.
• A small "Suggested" section under Favourites links to the developer's own free tools. It can be hidden with one tap.
